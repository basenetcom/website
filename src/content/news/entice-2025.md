## ENTICE 2025: Celebrating a Decade of Uplifting Tech Talents

ENTICE 2025 marked ten years of empowering Malaysia's technical talents. The event took place on 30 September 2025 at M Resort & Hotel, Kuala Lumpur, and was officiated by the Minister of Science, Technology and Innovation (MOSTI).

The milestone programme featured keynotes, interactive forums and national awards honouring outstanding technologists and technicians.
